📘 Multi-Agent Trading System Architecture & Guide

🤖 The 7 Specialized Agents

1. Market Agent

Fetches real-time quotes, trades, and daily/sub-minute OHLCV bars from Alpaca API with TTL caching.

2. Technical Agent

Computes technical indicators (RSI, MACD, EMA, Bollinger Bands, ATR, Volume ratios) across timeframes.

3. Fundamental Agent

Scans company profiles from Finnhub to evaluate P/E, EPS, Beta, and market cap valuation.

4. News Agent

Gathers company news headlines and calculates keyword-based sentiment (positive/negative/neutral).

5. Risk Agent

Assesses volatility (ATR% of price) and RSI overbought/oversold extreme warnings.

6. Portfolio Agent

Tracks cash, buying power, equity, and open position state to avoid churn.

7. Execution Agent

Combines agent scores (60%) and strategy ensemble (40%) to issue final BUY/SELL/HOLD decision with confidence %.

🗳️ The 5 Strategy Hypothesis Voters

Momentum

Evaluates price change %, volume surges, and RSI/MACD directional alignment.

Trend Following

Checks EMA(20) vs EMA(50) crossover, trend distance, and ATR dampening.

Mean Reversion

Identifies oversold/overbought RSI extremes and Bollinger Band touch reversals.

Breakout

Detects large price moves (>4%), volume surges (>2x), and Bollinger squeezes.

Swing

Combines RSI swing zones (30-45 buy, 55-70 sell), MACD cross, and portfolio state.
