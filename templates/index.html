📘 Multi-Agent Trading System Architecture & Guide

🤖 The 7 Specialized Agents

1. Market Agent

Fetches real-time quotes, trades, and daily/sub-minute OHLCV bars from Alpaca API with TTL caching.

2. Technical Agent

Computes technical indicators including RSI, MACD, EMA, Bollinger Bands, ATR, and volume ratios.

3. Fundamental Agent

Scans company profiles from Finnhub to evaluate P/E, EPS, Beta, and market capitalization.

4. News Agent

Gathers company news headlines and calculates keyword-based sentiment.

5. Risk Agent

Assesses volatility and RSI overbought/oversold warnings.

6. Portfolio Agent

Tracks cash, buying power, equity, and open positions to avoid unnecessary churn.

7. Execution Agent

Combines agent scores and strategy ensemble results to issue the final BUY/SELL/HOLD decision.

🗳️ The 5 Strategy Hypothesis Voters

Momentum

Evaluates price change, volume surges, and RSI/MACD directional alignment.

Trend Following

Checks EMA(20) versus EMA(50), trend distance, and ATR dampening.

Mean Reversion

Identifies oversold/overbought RSI extremes and Bollinger Band reversals.

Breakout

Detects large price moves, volume surges, and Bollinger squeezes.

Swing

Combines RSI swing zones, MACD crossovers, and portfolio state.
